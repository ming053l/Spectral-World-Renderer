Material-aware explicit representation

Compact Gaussian features predict local abundances at each ray–Gaussian intersection before alpha compositing. Shared visibility weights render spectra and material maps, keeping mixtures queryable on primitives across viewpoints.

Appearance-guided material allocation

Multi-view spectral-shape and brightness clusters guide abundance assignments without a material-label fitting loss, improving the separation of materials with similar spectral shapes.

Abstract

Hyperspectral images (HSI) preserve material evidence lost in RGB, yet 3D material identification from multi-view HSI faces two obstacles. Spectral Gaussian splatting reconstructs spectra accurately but leaves unmixing to per-pixel post-processing, while multi-view hyperspectral capture is costly and real datasets lack pixel-level material labels and sub-pixel proportions. We propose Spectral World Renderer (SWR), a material-aware explicit representation that unifies 3D spectral reconstruction and unmixing: it fits a Gaussian scaffold from RGB and decodes ray–Gaussian intersections into abundances over a shared endmember dictionary. Additionally, appearance guidance prevents spectrally similar materials from merging when intensity absorbs their brightness differences. Moreover, SWR requires no material-label supervision during training. To address the data gap, we further construct the SWR Benchmark (SWR-Bench): a coding-agent pipeline generates eight indoor and outdoor worlds from measured reflectance, aligning HSI with both annotations. SWR achieves the highest mIoU in all eight worlds and the highest mean PSNR on each real dataset. On an 11-class urban world, it trains at least 3.67× faster than unmixing baselines; rendering averages 34.9 joint spectral and label views/s over four worlds on a single RTX 4090.

Seeing beyond RGB

RGB847 nm

Stills from one camera path, rendered by SWR. Left: visible composite near 630 / 550 / 460 nm from the learned spectra. Right: a single band; both band views share one fixed exposure, so brightness is comparable between wavelengths. In the near-infrared, vegetation reflects strongly, whereas most building materials do not.

Living leaves differ from materials of the same colour beyond the visible range: chlorophyll absorbs red light and leaf tissue reflects near-infrared light, producing a red edge near 700 nm that minerals and dry leaves lack. In City, foliage-coloured mineral leaves are only 2° from foliage in RGB but 42° apart in spectrum, so they receive endmembers of their own.

Because abundances are stored on explicit, rasterizable Gaussians, a material query is a selection over primitives that is consistent across views and requires neither labels nor a per-image classifier. The mineral-leaf query retrieves no ordinary tree, whereas the equivalent RGB query retrieves eleven.

A measured spectrum can also serve as the query. With the sun and sky illumination factored out, a pixel clicked in shadow retrieves 77 % of the sunlit part of its material, against 10 % for an RGB query (mean over four worlds).

04 / Method

Unmixing inside rendering

Unmixing a rendered image afterwards acts on spectra that compositing has already mixed across surfaces. SWR instead unmixes inside rendering: at every ray intersection, a shared decoder turns the local feature into abundances and an intensity, and compositing accumulates both, so one render yields spectra and abundances.

SWR reconstruction and unmixing pipeline. An RGB scaffold first fits geometry and harmonic features; with geometry frozen, spectral mixtures are then learned. A shared decoder predicts intersection abundances and intensity over scene-wide endmembers. Shared visibility weights jointly render spectra and abundances. Appearance clusters initialize the dictionary and regularize abundance assignments.

Storage

Band-independent storage

An RGB-trained anchor provides compact geometry, and each Gaussian stores a feature instead of a spectrum, so storage does not grow with the number of bands.

Decoding

Decode before compositing

A shared decoder maps the feature at every ray intersection to abundances over a scene-wide endmember dictionary, so one render yields spectra and abundances and every Gaussian carries queryable abundances.

Guidance

Intensity and appearance guidance

A separate intensity absorbs shading, and multi-view appearance guidance assigns an over-complete set of endmembers to distinct appearances.

One Gaussian representation, composited with one set of visibility weights, renders spectra, abundances and material queries at any band count.

Multi-view appearance guidance

Appearance guidance separates similar materials. (a) Spectra of white marble and grey masonry. (b) Shape-and-brightness clusters pool training views. (c) Cluster assignments keep the two materials distinct in the test view.

Because the intensity absorbs brightness, a single endmember can explain two spectrally similar materials: in Forbidden City, white marble is four times brighter than grey masonry but only 3.8° apart in spectral shape.

Clustering the pixels of all training views by spectral shape and brightness separates such appearances, and every view shares the same clusters. Each cluster initializes its own endmember, and the assignment is retained as weak guidance during training, which gives marble its own layer.

05 / Results

Identification and fidelity

Mean scores from the paper on the eight SWR-Bench worlds. ↑ higher is better, ↓ lower is better.

SWR has the best mIoU in every world and learns the endmembers closest to the true spectra. Known composition exposes what fidelity hides: in Market and Study, 3DGS with post-hoc unmixing reaches a lower SAM but a far lower mIoU. Within SWR itself, decoding after compositing raises Market PSNR from 33.72 to 34.37 dB yet lowers mIoU from 0.47 to 0.37, the same gap.

Real captures

Without material ground truth, real captures test spectral fidelity. SWR has the best mean PSNR on each real source, by 1.55 dB on NeSpoF and 5.27 dB on the HSI-SC apple; HS-NeRF is nearly tied, and 3DGS reaches a lower mean and 95th-percentile angle there.
