Abstract摘要

Hyperspectral images (HSI) preserve material evidence lost in RGB, yet 3D material identification from multi-view HSI faces two obstacles. Spectral Gaussian splatting reconstructs spectra accurately but leaves unmixing to per-pixel post-processing, while multi-view hyperspectral capture is costly and real datasets lack pixel-level material labels and sub-pixel proportions. We propose Spectral World Renderer (SWR), a material-aware explicit representation that unifies 3D spectral reconstruction and unmixing: it fits a Gaussian scaffold from RGB and decodes ray–Gaussian intersections into abundances over a shared endmember dictionary. Additionally, appearance guidance prevents spectrally similar materials from merging when intensity absorbs their brightness differences. Moreover, SWR requires no material-label supervision during training. To address the data gap, we further construct the SWR Benchmark (SWR-Bench): a coding-agent pipeline generates eight indoor and outdoor worlds from measured reflectance, aligning HSI with both annotations. SWR achieves the highest mIoU in all eight worlds and the highest mean PSNR on each real dataset. On an 11-class urban world, it trains at least 3.67× faster than unmixing baselines; rendering averages 34.9 joint spectral and label views/s over four worlds on a single RTX 4090.

高光譜影像（HSI）保留了 RGB 中遺失的材質證據，然而從多視角 HSI 進行三維材質辨識面臨兩項障礙。光譜高斯潑濺能準確重建光譜，卻將解混留給逐像素的後處理；同時，多視角高光譜擷取成本高昂，且真實資料集缺乏像素級材質標籤與次像素比例。我們提出 Spectral World Renderer（SWR），一種具材質感知、統一三維光譜重建與解混的顯式表示：它從 RGB 擬合高斯骨架，並將光線–高斯交點解碼為共享端元字典上的豐度。此外，當強度吸收了亮度差異時，外觀引導可防止光譜相近的材質被合併。SWR 在訓練期間亦不需要材質標籤監督。為彌補資料缺口，我們進一步建構 SWR Benchmark（SWR-Bench）：以編碼代理流程從實測反射率生成八個室內與室外世界，使 HSI 與上述兩種標註對齊。SWR 在全部八個世界中取得最高的 mIoU，並在每個真實資料集上取得最高的平均 PSNR。在一個 11 類的城市世界中，其訓練速度至少為解混基線的 3.67 倍；在單張 RTX 4090 上，四個世界的平均渲染速度為每秒 34.9 個聯合光譜與標籤視角。

Living leaves differ from materials of the same colour beyond the visible range: chlorophyll absorbs red light and leaf tissue reflects near-infrared light, producing a red edge near 700 nm that minerals and dry leaves lack. In City, foliage-coloured mineral leaves are only 2° from foliage in RGB but 42° apart in spectrum, so they receive endmembers of their own.活體葉片與同色材質的差異出現在可見光範圍之外：葉綠素吸收紅光，葉片組織反射近紅外光，在 700 nm 附近形成礦物與枯葉所沒有的紅邊。在 City 中，與植被同色的礦物葉片在 RGB 中與植被僅相差 2°，在光譜上卻相差 42°，因此各自獲得獨立的端元。

Because abundances are stored on explicit, rasterizable Gaussians, a material query is a selection over primitives that is consistent across views and requires neither labels nor a per-image classifier. 04 / Method方法

Unmixing inside rendering在渲染過程中解混

Unmixing a rendered image afterwards acts on spectra that compositing has already mixed across surfaces. SWR instead unmixes inside rendering: at every ray intersection, a shared decoder turns the local feature into abundances and an intensity, and compositing accumulates both, so one render yields spectra and abundances.對渲染後的影像再進行解混，所處理的光譜已在合成時跨表面混合。SWR 則在渲染過程中解混：在每個光線交點，共享解碼器將局部特徵轉換為豐度與強度，合成時同時累積兩者，因此一次渲染即可得到光譜與豐度。

SWR reconstruction and unmixing pipeline. An RGB scaffold first fits geometry and harmonic features; with geometry frozen, spectral mixtures are then learned. A shared decoder predicts intersection abundances and intensity over scene-wide endmembers. Shared visibility weights jointly render spectra and abundances. Appearance clusters initialize the dictionary and regularize abundance assignments.SWR 重建與解混流程。RGB 骨架先擬合幾何與諧波特徵；固定幾何後再學習光譜混合。共享解碼器在交點處預測相對於全場景端元的豐度與強度。共用的可見度權重聯合渲染光譜與豐度。外觀聚類用以初始化字典並正則化豐度分配。

Storage儲存

Band-independent storage與波段數無關的儲存

An RGB-trained anchor provides compact geometry, and each Gaussian stores a feature instead of a spectrum, so storage does not grow with the number of bands.以 RGB 訓練的錨點提供精簡的幾何，每個高斯儲存特徵而非光譜，因此儲存量不隨波段數增加。

Decoding解碼

Decode before compositing先解碼，再合成

A shared decoder maps the feature at every ray intersection to abundances over a scene-wide endmember dictionary, so one render yields spectra and abundances and every Gaussian carries queryable abundances.共享解碼器將每個光線交點的特徵映射為全場景端元字典上的豐度，因此一次渲染即得到光譜與豐度，且每個高斯都帶有可查詢的豐度。

Guidance引導

Intensity and appearance guidance強度與外觀引導

A separate intensity absorbs shading, and multi-view appearance guidance assigns an over-complete set of endmembers to distinct appearances.獨立的強度項吸收明暗變化，多視角外觀引導則將過完備的端元集合分配給不同的外觀。

One Gaussian representation, composited with one set of visibility weights, renders spectra, abundances and material queries at any band count.單一高斯表示以同一組可見度權重合成，即可在任意波段數下渲染光譜、豐度與材質查詢。

Multi-view appearance guidance多視角外觀引導

Appearance guidance separates similar materials. (a) Spectra of white marble and grey masonry. (b) Shape-and-brightness clusters pool training views. (c) Cluster assignments keep the two materials distinct in the test view.外觀引導分離相似材質。(a) white marble 與 grey masonry 的光譜。(b) 光譜形狀與亮度聚類彙整所有訓練視角。(c) 聚類分配使兩種材質在測試視角中保持區分。

Because the intensity absorbs brightness, a single endmember can explain two spectrally similar materials: in Forbidden City, white marble is four times brighter than grey masonry but only 3.8° apart in spectral shape.由於強度吸收了亮度，單一端元可能同時解釋兩種光譜相近的材質：在 Forbidden City 中，white marble 的亮度是 grey masonry 的四倍，光譜形狀卻僅相差 3.8°。

Clustering the pixels of all training views by spectral shape and brightness separates such appearances, and every view shares the same clusters. Each cluster initializes its own endmember, and the assignment is retained as weak guidance during training, which gives marble its own layer.依光譜形狀與亮度對所有訓練視角的像素進行聚類，即可分離這類外觀，且所有視角共用相同的聚類。每個聚類初始化各自的端元，其分配在訓練期間保留為弱引導，使 marble 得到獨立的圖層。

Throughput is the mean over Market, Botanical, Study and Karst.SWR 使用最佳化的融合 fp16 解碼器，略過權重低於 10−4 的交點（標籤 99.97 % 相同）；基線方法使用其參考程式碼。吞吐量為 Market、Botanical、Study 與 Karst 的平均。

Real captures真實擷取資料

Without material ground truth, real captures test spectral fidelity. SWR has the best mean PSNR on each real source, by 1.55 dB on NeSpoF and 5.27 dB on the HSI-SC apple; HS-NeRF is nearly tied, and 3DGS reaches a lower mean and 95th-percentile angle there.在沒有材質真值的情況下，真實擷取資料用以檢驗光譜保真度。SWR 在每個真實資料來源上都取得最佳平均 PSNR，在 NeSpoF 上高出 1.55 dB，在 HSI-SC apple 上高出 5.27 dB；在 HS-NeRF 上兩者幾乎持平，且 3DGS 在該資料上的平均與第 95 百分位光譜角較低。

Related work相關研究

Spectral World Renderer builds on and compares with these works on spectral unmixing, hyperspectral reconstruction, 3D scene queries and material recognition.Spectral World Renderer 延伸並比較了以下在光譜解混、高光譜重建、三維場景查詢與材質辨識方面的研究。

  • ICCV 2025UnMix-NeRF: Spectral Unmixing Meets Neural Radiance FieldsSpectral unmixing inside a neural radiance field; the material-identification baseline on SWR-Bench.在神經輻射場中進行光譜解混；SWR-Bench 上的材質辨識基線。
  • CVPR 2025HyperGS: Hyperspectral 3D Gaussian SplattingHyperspectral Gaussian splatting through a latent spectral representation; a fidelity baseline on real captures.透過潛在光譜表示進行高光譜高斯潑濺；真實擷取資料上的保真度基線。
  • ECCV 2026Neural Harmonic Textures for High-Quality Primitive Based Neural ReconstructionHarmonic features interpolated within each primitive; the feature field that SWR decodes into abundances.在每個基元內內插的諧波特徵；SWR 將此特徵場解碼為豐度。
  • ICCV 2023LERF: Language Embedded Radiance FieldsOpen-vocabulary queries lifted into 3D; the query interface that material queries follow.將開放詞彙查詢提升至三維；材質查詢沿用此查詢介面。
  • CVPR 2024LangSplat: 3D Language Gaussian SplattingLanguage features attached to 3D Gaussians for fast open-vocabulary queries; material queries are likewise selections over Gaussians, answered by abundances and spectra.將語言特徵附加於三維高斯以進行快速的開放詞彙查詢；材質查詢同樣是對高斯的選取，並由豐度與光譜回答。
  • 3DV 2026SAMa: Material-Aware 3D Selection and SegmentationMaterial selection in 3D from RGB views; spectra additionally separate surfaces of the same colour but different composition.從 RGB 視角在三維中選取材質；光譜可進一步分離顏色相同但組成不同的表面。
  • ECCV 2022A Dense Material Segmentation Dataset for Indoor and Outdoor Scene ParsingPer-pixel material categories from RGB images; SWR instead estimates sub-pixel material proportions.從 RGB 影像得到逐像素的材質類別；SWR 則估計次像素的材質比例。
